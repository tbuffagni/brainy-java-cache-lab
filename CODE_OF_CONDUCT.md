# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Brainy Java Cache Lab must remain respectful, evidence-based, and welcoming. Disagreement is encouraged when it improves the experiment, but criticism must address methods, data, code, or claims rather than individuals.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- explaining technical objections with enough detail to evaluate them;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes transparently;
- respecting different levels of experience;
- avoiding harassment, insults, discrimination, and disclosure of private information;
- giving appropriate credit for reproductions, corrections, and contributions.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit comments, and other public spaces associated with the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Public technical disagreement should remain in the relevant issue or discussion. Conduct concerns that should not be reported publicly may be sent to the maintainer through a private message on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thomasbuffagni/).

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical.
